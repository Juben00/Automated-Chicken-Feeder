How to calibrate:
  1. Enter your Raspberry Pi's URL above and save it.
  2. Place a container on a scale under the feeder valve.
  3. Set the test duration and click Run Calibration Test.
  4. Weigh the dispensed feed in grams.
  5. Calculate: grams ÷ seconds = grams per second
  6. Enter the result in Grams per second on the left and save.
