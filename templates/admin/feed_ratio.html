The valve will open for this many seconds, then close. Max 30s.
How to calibrate:
  1. Enter your Raspberry Pi's URL above (e.g. http://192.168.1.100:5000) and save it.
  2. Place a container on a scale under the feeder valve.
  3. Set the test duration (e.g. 5 or 10 seconds) and click Run Calibration Test.
  4. Weigh the dispensed feed in grams.
  5. Calculate: grams ÷ seconds = grams per second
  6. Enter the result in the Grams per second field above and save.
